New react_range in [unit.NAME] / [building.NAME] (0 to 20, -1 = the game's): how far a unit looks for a target on its own, separate from how far it can shoot. A range above the game's reaction distance now raises the reaction distance to match (never lowers it), so a tower given range 9 opens fire at 9 instead of its old 6. Ships and land units already notice enemies further than they shoot, so their range alone was working.
