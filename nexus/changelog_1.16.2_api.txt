Fixed a crash: a paladin given Heal by autocast while it still had an attack-move to carry out (Remastered resumes such orders after a cast) crashed the game inside the Heal action; Flame Shield had the same hole. Every order the mod gives now clears the pending attack-move or patrol first, exactly as your own clicks do. A caster that autocasts therefore no longer resumes its attack-move afterwards; it stands and fights where it is.
