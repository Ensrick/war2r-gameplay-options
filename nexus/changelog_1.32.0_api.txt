Blizzard and Death and Decay now value what they hit: guard and cannon towers count 3x, barracks, halls and other production buildings, casters and siege 1.5x, farms, walls, scout towers and oil platforms 0.3x ([area_values] changes any of them).
No more wasted Blizzards on a pair of farms when a tower or an army is a few tiles further: when the best spot in reach is worth less than area_settle_percent (50) of the best one within lookahead_tiles (8) more, the caster waits; move it a few steps closer. The log says what it waits for.
Area spells come first: while a target worth area_reserve_value (4, one barracks or four units) is that close, the caster keeps the mana for one full Blizzard or Death and Decay and only spends the rest on other spells.
