[size=4][b]1.0.2[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Quieter defaults: out of the box only Heal, Slow, Bloodlust and Raise Dead autocast, plus worker auto-repair. Exorcism, Polymorph, Death Coil, Haste, Eye of Kilrogg, worker auto-harvest and hero regeneration are now off until you switch them on.
[*]The unit examples in the config (sight 8 for dragons and gryphon riders, the tweaked destroyer) are comments now instead of active settings.
[*]Updating from 1.0.0 / 1.0.1: your existing gameplay_options.toml is not overwritten and keeps behaving as before. Delete it if you want the new defaults.
[/list]

[size=4][b]1.0.1[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]New [building.name] tables: set hit points, armor, damage, range, sight, gold, lumber, oil and build time for any structure, the same way [unit.name] works for units. Tower names moved there.
[*]New health multipliers for structures: buildings and building_upgrades under [health.human] / [health.orc]. The "all" values stay unit-only.
[*]Unit price multipliers now scale oil as well as gold and lumber.
[*]The [heroes] list is grouped by side, and unit names accept short spellings (uther, grom, teron, korgath, gryphon).
[/list]

[size=4][b]1.0.0 - Initial release[/b][/size]
For Warcraft II: Remastered 1.0.2.2818, single-player.

[b]Autocast[/b]
[list]
[*]Paladin: Heal (units missing 10+ hit points, most hurt first), Exorcism.
[*]Mage: Polymorph with a configurable, prioritised target list (dragons, gryphon riders and daemons first), Slow.
[*]Ogre-Mage: Bloodlust on units that are fighting; Eye of Kilrogg when idle at full mana, and the eye scouts unexplored ground by itself.
[*]Death Knight: Raise Dead when enemies are near, Death Coil, Haste on flyers, Unholy Armor (off by default).
[*]Move orders are never interrupted, casters never double up on a target, Ctrl+F9 toggles autocast in game.
[*]Left manual on purpose: Fireball, Flame Shield, Invisibility, Blizzard, Death and Decay, Whirlwind, Runes.
[/list]

[b]Workers[/b]
[list]
[*]Idle workers repair damaged buildings nearby (1 second, 10 tiles), then return to the nearest gold mine or tree (10 seconds, 5 tiles). Loaded workers deliver first. Stand Ground is respected.
[/list]

[b]Options[/b]
[list]
[*]Unlimited gold mines, off by default.
[*]Heroes regenerate 1 hit point per second.
[*]Multipliers for health, prices and build / research time: master x race (human / orc) x group, all 1.0 by default. Health only touches units.
[*]Per-unit base stats in [unit.name] tables: hit points, armor, basic and piercing damage, range, sight, gold, lumber, oil, build time. Ships with sight 8 for dragons and gryphon riders and a worked destroyer example.
[*]Configurable range bonus for Longbow / Lighter Axes.
[/list]

[b]Config[/b]
[list]
[*]gameplay_options.toml, re-read while the game runs, with error reporting that never breaks a running game.
[/list]
